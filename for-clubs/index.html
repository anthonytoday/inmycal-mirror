    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. No account, no contract, no ads in the widget.

    What do fans get?

    Follow subscribes them to every match of your team, and it stays up to date by itself. Tapping + on one match adds only that match, as a one-time event.

    Does it slow down our site?

    No. The widget loads in its own frame, weighs a few kilobytes, sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

    Can it match our colors?

    Light, dark or automatic today. For a custom design or your own competitions, email ops@inmycal.com.

    Which teams are available?

    Every club in the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1, and 55 European national teams.
