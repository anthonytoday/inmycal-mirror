    Add sports fixtures to your calendar

    InMyCal puts the 2026-27 fixtures of the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and national teams, plus the UFC and Formula 1 schedules, straight into your calendar.

    Pick your team once and every match appears in Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac), Google Calendar or Outlook, with kickoff times in your own time zone. When a kickoff moves, your calendar updates by itself. Free, no account, no tracking.
